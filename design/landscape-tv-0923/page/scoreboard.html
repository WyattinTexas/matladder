Design pass · 9/23 · for Wyatt's wordFOOTWORK, sideways: THE SCOREBOARD

Turn the phone and FOOTWORK becomes a scoreboard for the room. One giant clock, the session ladder beside it with the gap to your best on every row, the group's top four along the bottom, and a gold moment when a best falls. Portrait stays exactly as it is. Card 1210 is queued to build it and put build 7 on TestFlight tonight; the questions at the end are where your word changes the build.

What landscape is today the read · nothing is designed for it

There is no landscape layout in the app. Sideways, the phone shows the same 448-pixel column: chips, the demo clip and the clock fill the height, the times fall below the fold, and while the clock runs the whole screen empties. Mirrored to a TV, that is a thin strip with 12-pixel times.

The design phone-landscape stills, 844×390 at 3×; the TV shows the same frame

Recommendations the card goes with all twelve unless you say otherwise

  • R1
    Auto scoreboard. Landscape switches the stage on; no toggle, no setting. Portrait is byte-identical to today.
  • R2
    Dark stage, not white. A white 1080p mirror is a floodlight in a gym. Navy reads from the mat and makes the gold moment land. Portrait keeps the white brand.
  • R3
    The ladder never disappears. Running changes the digit colour and the button, nothing else. Today's hide-everything is the biggest loss on a TV.
  • R4
    Every row carries a delta. +0.19 against the session best. On a TV the delta is the story: the gap closing.
  • R5
    The NEW BEST moment. Gold banner for 2.5 s, clock and row go gold, one soft flash. No confetti, no emoji. Fires for a session best and for group #1 today.
  • R6
    Group strip in the footer. Top four today for this drill, live from the group; you outlined in blue. Solo players see their own bests: today, this week, all time.
  • R7
    Leaderboard and streaks as a side panel at 46% width with a shade. Same content, same period chips.
  • R8
    Big tap zone. The whole clock column starts and stops the clock, plus the rail button. Nobody hunts for an 88-pixel dot while looking at the TV.
  • R9
    Setup in two columns with the button in view.
  • R10
    The screen stays awake in the stage (wake lock; the native KeepAwake plugin is the real fix, Q5).
  • R11
    The demo clip stays a portrait thing. Sideways, tapping the drill name opens the picker as a sheet, and the clip lives there.
  • R12
    Nothing else moves. Chart, heat map, warm-up, box breathing, finish flow keep their behaviour and scale by screen height.

The Road marathon training mode · added on your word tonight · card 1212

RUN is a mode chip at the front of the drill picker, so runners and ladder athletes share the same app and the same group. Location is asked for only at the first START in RUN, in plain words. Distance and pace come from the phone's GPS, every mile is split exactly at the crossing, and the voice calls it the way the fitness app does. The route never leaves the phone; only time, distance and splits go to the group.
